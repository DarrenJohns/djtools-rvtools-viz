📘 How this works

Here we match your VMs to Azure VM sizes and provide initial pricing guidance. Work through the three steps below to go from raw inventory to a costed, exportable plan.

  1. 1 🏷️ Classify your VMs — apply tags using workload rules, Auto tag assignment rules, or manual 🏷️ Tag selected, so similar VMs are easy to find and group together.
  2. 2 📁 Group similar VMs to find the best match on Azure — use ✨ Auto-group by tag to slice the inventory in one go, or tick VMs in the grid and use ➕ New manual group to hand-pick. Each group shows a recommended Azure SKU and monthly price.
  3. 3 📊 Land on a confident estimate you can share — adjust each group's sizing constraints to narrow down the Azure VM match, review the monthly price, then export to Excel (all groups, or just the pinned ones).

💡 Tip: hit 💾 Save workspace at any time to download a .rvz bundle of your data, tags, rules and groups — re-import it later from the Import tab to pick up exactly where you left off.
